# Hosting and Scaling GenAI Workloads on Amazon EKS

<p class="cc-byline" markdown>
**AWS workshop** &middot; Monday 9 November 2026, 9:30 am &ndash; 12:00 pm &middot; Cockle Bay Room 1<br>Facilitated by Frank Fan, Sheng Chen, Navya Srivastava and Parikshit Patel
</p>

*Pre-conference workshop (2.5 hours)*

## Abstract

Deploy, serve, and scale GenAI models on Amazon EKS with NVIDIA GPUs. This workshop covers GPU cluster setup, model serving with vLLM, observability, and your choice of scaling with Ray Serve or inference optimization with KV cache offloading.

## What the workshop covers

- **GPU cluster setup** &mdash; stand up GPU-enabled Amazon EKS clusters for GenAI workloads.
- **Model serving with vLLM** &mdash; deploy and serve models on NVIDIA GPUs.
- **Observability** &mdash; instrument and observe inference workloads.
- **Scaling and optimization** &mdash; scale out with Ray Serve, or optimize inference with KV cache offloading.
