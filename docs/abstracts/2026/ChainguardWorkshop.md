# Break It, Fix It: Hands-On Container and Library Supply Chain Security

<p class="cc-byline" markdown>
**Chainguard workshop** &middot; Monday 9 November 2026, 1:30 pm &ndash; 4:00 pm &middot; Cockle Bay Room<br>Facilitated by Andrew Dean
</p>

*Pre-conference workshop (2.5 hours)*

## Abstract

Scan a Kubernetes stack, count the CVEs, then fix them. In this hands-on lab, you will migrate third-party and custom images to hardened bases, migrate to a malware-free library endpoint, exploit and then remediate a vulnerable library, and enforce image signing. Each attendee gets a dedicated VM and Kubernetes cluster.

## Why attend?

Move from vulnerability reports to practical remediation. Work through a series of challenges in your own Kubernetes environment, measure the impact of each change and see how trusted images and libraries fit into your existing workflows.

You'll leave with experience you can apply to your own workloads, from hardening a custom application to enforcing which images can run in your cluster.

## What you will leave with

- Practical experience migrating third-party workloads and custom applications to hardened images.
- A repeatable process for scanning, prioritising, remediating and validating vulnerabilities.
- An understanding of trusted dependency sourcing and library backporting.
- Experience enforcing image signing at deployment.
- Measurable before-and-after results from your own lab environment.

## Facilitator

- **Andrew Dean** &middot; Solutions Architect, Chainguard

## Prerequisites

Bring your own laptop.
