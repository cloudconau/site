# AI and platform maturity: the foundations that help AI deliver

<p class="cc-byline" markdown>
**Charlotte Fleming** &middot; Researcher, Developer Relations, Octopus Deploy
</p>

*Lightning Talk (5 mins)*

## Abstract

AI has spread rapidly across development and delivery practices, with the benefits teams report differing widely. The question worth asking is what separates the teams seeing real gains from those left with more noise than value. Drawing on Octopus Deploy's Future of Platform Engineering research report, alongside the broader AI literature, this talk makes the case that the platform foundation beneath AI determines whether it pays off.

We found that the more mature platforms are also the ones reporting positive AI impact on software delivery speed and stability, organizational performance, and strong positive associations with job satisfaction and leadership direction. This aligns with DORA's finding that AI amplifies existing strengths when added to a well-designed system, compounding its value.

The more interesting finding is where that impact does and doesn't reach. Teams reporting a positive impact of AI on delivery speed and stability, legacy modernization, and organizational performance are more likely to be high performers (i.e., delivering on goals). But that same benefit did not extend to how work actually feels: reported AI impact showed no comparable improvement in work-life balance or wellbeing (i.e., burnout). Therefore, the gains are landing on delivery and organizational outcomes, but not on the people actually doing the work, which is worth noting.

The session connects these findings in broader research, providing a clear perspective on where AI investments are actually delivering results.

Topics include:

- The positive impact of AI on delivery speed and stability, and organizational performance for mature platforms, correlates with platform goal delivery.
- The association between AI impact and higher job satisfaction and leadership direction, but not with well-being or work-life balance.

## Speaker Bio

Charlotte Fleming is a Researcher in Developer Relations at Octopus Deploy, where she leads research into AI adoption, Platform Engineering, and Continuous Delivery practices. With a PhD in Neuroscience, she brings analytical precision to software development research. She authors Octopus Deploy's AI Pulse and Platform Engineering Pulse reports.
