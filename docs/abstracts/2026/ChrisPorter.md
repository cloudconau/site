# Cloud Native Global Load Balancing with K8gb

<p class="cc-byline" markdown>
**Chris Porter** &middot; Principal Solutions Architect – Cloud Native, Nutanix
</p>

*Full length talk (20 minutes)*

## Abstract

In this session, learn how organisations big and small can provide availability for Kubernetes apps between multiple datacenters, cities and even across the globe!

k8gb is an open source global load balancer that recently moved from CNCF sandbox to incubating. Its a powerful tool that works seamlessly with kubernetes resources to provide availability for disperse environments.

## Speaker Bio

Chris Porter (he/him) is a Brit masquerading as an Australian, who specialises in cloud native technologies as a Principal Solutions Architect at Nutanix. Despite his British roots, he can correctly pronounces "data" and "router", but struggles with the use of Z instead of S in words such as "specialises". Chris' career in IT spans many decades, working in both end-user and vendor roles, including a 4 year stint at AWS prior to joining Nutanix.
