# Cloud Native Global Load Balancing with K8gb

<p class="cc-byline" markdown>
**Woh Shon Phoon** &middot; Solutions Architect, Nutanix
</p>

*Full length talk (20 minutes)*

## Abstract

In this session, learn how organisations big and small can provide availability for Kubernetes apps between multiple datacenters, cities and even across the globe!

k8gb is an open source global load balancer that recently moved from CNCF sandbox to incubating. Its a powerful tool that works seamlessly with kubernetes resources to provide availability for disperse environments.

## Speaker Bio

Woh Shon is a Solutions Architect at Nutanix APJ, focusing on Cloud-Native, AI/ML, and automation. Throughout his career, he has held technical and leadership roles across both enterprise end-user environments and technology product companies. A hands-on practitioner at heart, he bridges high-level design with day-to-day technical realities, focusing on practical implementation and reliable modern architectures.
