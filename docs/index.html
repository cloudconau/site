ABOUT

About CloudCon Sydney

CloudCon Sydney 2026 is organised by KCD Sydney Incorporated, the local chapter of the global Kubernetes Community Days program backed by the CNCF. It brings together over 200 cloud-native practitioners from across Australia and Asia-Pacific for two days of real-world talks, hands-on workshops, and community networking at ICC Sydney.

Our program is community-driven and practitioner-focused. Expect frank conversations about what's working and what isn't in production cloud-native environments today.

Who attends?

  • → Software, Platform, DevOps & SRE Engineers
  • → Cloud & Solutions Architects
  • → Technical Leaders & Engineering Managers
  • → Security Professionals & Cloud Security Specialists
  • → AI/ML Engineers & Data Scientists

PROGRAM

Conference Topics

Five focus areas covering the full spectrum of cloud-native technology.

⎈
Kubernetes
Cluster operations, release cycles, GitOps, scaling patterns, and Kubernetes-native tooling in production.
🤖
AI & ML
AI agents on Kubernetes, LLM inference, MLOps, agentic platforms, and GenAI observability.
🏗️
Platform Engineering
Internal developer platforms, golden paths, developer experience, and platform-as-a-product thinking.
🔒
Security
Cloud security, supply chain security, policy-as-code, compliance frameworks, and threat detection.
💡
Other
Cloud cost optimisation, observability platforms, cryptography, and cross-cutting cloud-native topics.
